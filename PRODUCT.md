# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AlgoPeer serves teachers and students in an Algorithm Design and Analysis course. Teachers configure coursework, monitor submissions and cross-class review, grade with evidence, resolve exceptions, and publish auditable results. Students submit work and, when designated, review the other class anonymously.

## Product Purpose

Provide a reliable end-to-end course workflow from assignment publication through submission, fixed five-person cross-class review, teacher grading, and result publication. Success means every score is explainable, permissions prevent identity or grade leakage, failures are recoverable, and a teacher can complete the workflow without technical knowledge.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, and dependable. The product should feel like a well-run academic workspace: information-dense when needed, plain-spoken, and never theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative analytics dashboards, neon AI aesthetics, glassmorphism, oversized vanity metrics, and prototype screens with dead controls. Do not make automated analysis appear more authoritative than the evidence behind it.

## Design Principles

1. Put the next valid action in front of the teacher and explain why blocked actions are blocked.
2. Keep original evidence, derived analysis, teacher decisions, and published grades visibly distinct.
3. Make cross-class review rules inspectable instead of hiding them behind automation.
4. Prefer recoverable background workflows with status and retry over synchronous waiting.
5. Treat privacy, audit history, and failure states as primary product features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core workflows must be keyboard operable, retain visible focus, avoid color-only state communication, respect reduced motion, and use clear Simplified Chinese labels and error messages.
